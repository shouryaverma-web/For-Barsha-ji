Dear Barsha, ❤️

Barsha, tum meri bahut achhi dost thi, aur ek waqt aisa bhi tha jab maine tumhe khone ki umeed mein apni saari umeedein kho di thi, lekin aaj tum phir se meri zindagi mein ho aur woh umeed dobara jaag gayi hai.

Tum kabhi meri crush thi, lekin shayad himmat nahi juta paaya ki tumhe apne dil ki baat bata sakun. Aaj bhi darr lagta hai tumhe khone se, kyunki tumhare liye jo feelings hain, woh aaj bhi waisi hi hain.

Main nahi jaanta meri kahani aage kahan tak jaayegi, na hi ye jaanta hoon ki humara future kya hoga, lekin itna zaroor jaanta hoon ki tum meri kahani ka ek bahut khoobsurat hissa ban chuki ho.

Hum online mile, humari baatein shayad zyada nahi hui, lekin pata nahi kyun tumse ek alag hi attachment ho gayi. Shayad 1.5 saal pehle kisi aur ke liye kabhi aisi feelings thi, lekin aaj jo ehsaas tumhare liye hai, woh mere liye bahut khaas hai.

Main tumse koi bade-bade vaade nahi karna chahta, bas itna chahta hoon ki agar kismat ne humein ek saath likha hai, toh main tumhare saath apni poori zindagi ki kahani likhna chahta hoon.

Thank you, Barsha, meri zindagi mein dobara woh khushi aur umeed laane ke liye. Thank you meri kahani ka itna khoobsurat hissa banne ke liye.

Aur aakhir mein bas ek sawaal... Kya tum meri kahani ka hissa bankar, meri humsafar banogi? Kya tum mujhse shaadi karogi? ❤️

I Love You, Barsha. ❤️
